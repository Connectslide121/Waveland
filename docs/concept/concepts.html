Waveland · three looks

The same platformer moment in three styles, from loudest to calmest. Each one is drawn in code and moves to the same 120 BPM beat: platforms with the bass, the bridge on the snare, spikes on the off-beats.

1

Neon arcade

Intensity

WAVELAND

Loud and glowing. Thick bloom, saturated magenta, cyan and violet, pulse rings on every kick. The most "party", like Geometry Dash meets synthwave. Risk: it can tire the eyes over a long session, and the drops have less room to feel bigger.

2

Wireframe

Intensity
Midnight Drive · 120 BPM
048 210
WAVELAND

Calm and precise. Thin white outlines on black, like a vector display or an oscilloscope. Only two colours carry meaning: cyan is you and safe paths, red is danger. Easy on the eyes and very readable, and it leaves lots of room for the drops to explode into colour as a contrast.

3

Dusk

Intensity
Midnight Drive · 120 BPM
48 210
waveland

Warm and dreamy. A sunset sky with a striped sun that breathes with the kick, soft rounded pastel platforms and a cream player, like Alto's Odyssey or Sayonara Wild Hearts. Still geometric and drawn in code, but gentle. Each song could pick its own time of day from its mood.
